Shipment Policy

Shipment Policy

At Weighvox, we are committed to delivering your orders promptly and safely. Please review our shipment policy for details on processing, shipping, and delivery.

Order Processing Times

  • All orders are processed within 1-3 business days (excluding weekends and holidays) after receiving your order confirmation email.
  • You will receive a notification when your order has shipped.
  • Orders placed after 2 PM will be processed on the next business day.

Shipping Methods and Delivery Timess

  • Standard Shipping (5-7 business days)
  • Expedited Shipping (2-3 business days)
  • Overnight Shipping (1 business day)
  • Delivery times vary based on your location and the shipping method selected at checkout.

Shipping Costss

  • Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on the weight, dimensions of the items, and the selected shipping method.
  • Free shipping is available on orders over $100 (Standard Shipping only).

Shipment Trackings

  • Once your order is shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number that allows you to monitor the status of your delivery.
  • If you do not receive a tracking number, please contact our customer service team at [dxb@weighvox.com].

International Shippings

  • Weighvox offers international shipping to selected countries.
  • Delivery times for international shipments vary based on the destination and customs processing. Please allow 10-15 business days for international deliveries.
  • Customs fees, taxes, and duties for international orders are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the shipping cost.

Delayed or Lost Shipmentss

  • All orders are processed within 1-3 business days (excluding weekends and holidays) after receiving your order confirmation email.
  • You will receive a notification when your order has shipped.
  • Orders placed after 2 PM will be processed on the next business day.
